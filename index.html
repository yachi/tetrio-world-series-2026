TETR.IO World Series 2026: round-by-round stats

32 broadcast matches, 1401 rounds. Click a match to see every round: its timestamp, winner and each player's VS, APP, PPS, APM, attack, pieces and time. Matches with 0 in "Rounds with stats" have winners and timestamps only.

Unofficial. Read automatically from the broadcast overlay, with every round winner checked by hand against the on-screen board and score bar. Small reading errors are possible. VS is the overlay's VS score. APP = attack ÷ pieces, rounded down to 2 decimals. PPS = pieces per second. APM = attack per minute, computed as attack ÷ round time. Where the round time was not read, it is estimated as pieces ÷ PPS. For five broadcasts the overlay could not be read at stream quality, so their stats were read from the 1080p frame at FINISH and checked against their own rates (pieces ÷ time = PPS, attack ÷ time = APM). There the losing player's numbers are red and clear, but the winner's are often white on a light background; where they could not be read the row says “not legible”.
