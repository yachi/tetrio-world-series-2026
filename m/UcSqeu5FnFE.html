Unofficial. Read automatically from the broadcast overlay, with every round winner checked by hand against the on-screen board and score bar. Small reading errors are possible. VS is the overlay's VS score. APP = attack ÷ pieces, rounded down to 2 decimals. PPS = pieces per second. APM = attack per minute, computed as attack ÷ round time. Where the round time was not read, it is estimated as pieces ÷ PPS. For five broadcasts the overlay could not be read at stream quality, so their stats were read from the 1080p frame at FINISH and checked against their own rates (pieces ÷ time = PPS, attack ÷ time = APM). There the losing player's numbers are red and clear, but the winner's are often white on a light background and covered by effects at FINISH. Winner numbers marked “≈” are estimates: the last reading that checks against its own rates in the seconds before FINISH, scaled to the round's length (the loser's time). Where even that was not possible the row says “not legible”.
